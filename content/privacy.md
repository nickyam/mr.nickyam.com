**Last updated: 25 November 2026**

This site, `mr.nickyam.com` ("Mr.Yam", "we", "us"), is a personal blog about Bitcoin, crypto, technology and the occasional note about the world. This page explains what happens to data when you read it. It is deliberately short, because we collect very little.

## What we do not collect

- There are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no comment system. You cannot register here, and we do not build profiles of readers.
- We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party behavioural tracking script.
- We do not sell, rent or trade any data. There is nothing to sell.

## What is processed automatically

Like every website, our hosting provider processes the technical request needed to deliver a page: IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp. This site is served by **Cloudflare Pages**, so those requests are handled by Cloudflare's edge network under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Server logs are kept by the provider for security and abuse prevention, not for marketing.

If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply to you.

## Cookies and local storage

This site itself sets **no tracking cookies**. Two things are stored in your browser:

1. **`pref-theme`** — a `localStorage` entry that remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. It never leaves your browser.
2. **Advertising cookies** — set by Google when ads are shown (see below), not by us.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays advertising through **Google AdSense** (publisher ID `pub-7114121088728794`).

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- For readers in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent management platform asks for your permission before personalised ads are served, as required by GDPR / UK GDPR. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time through the consent message on the site.
- Google's own explanation of how it uses information from sites that use its services: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Images and external links

Article images are hosted on our own image host, `yun.nickyam.com`, so reading a page does not ping a third party for pictures. Some articles link out to external sources (news sites, research papers, block explorers). Once you follow such a link, the destination site's own privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California or another jurisdiction with data-protection law, you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, and to object to processing. In practice we hold almost nothing beyond an email if you wrote to us.

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask a question about this policy, write to **me@nickyam.com**.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially — for example if an analytics service or a newsletter is added — the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published on this page.
